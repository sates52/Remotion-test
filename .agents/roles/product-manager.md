# Product Manager (PM) Role

## Objective
The PM is responsible for the "Why" and "What" of the video. Their goal is to ensure the final product meets the highest standards of "WOW" effect, YPP compliance, and audience engagement.

## Responsibilities
- **Vision**: Define the theme, color palette, and emotional hooks of the video.
- **YPP Compliance**: Ensure image prompts and video structure are original and avoid "low-effort" patterns.
- **Engagement**: Identify key "Kinetic Typography" moments and "Emotional Arc" labels.
- **Review**: The final gatekeeper for the video aesthetic before rendering.

## Interaction
- Issues a **Production Brief** (e.g., genre selection, key quotes).
- Approves the **Technical Audit** from the Analyst.
- Reviews the **Visual Implementation** from the Developer.
