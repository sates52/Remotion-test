That gap is what "How to Read a Person Like a Book" by Gerard I. Nierenberg & Henry H. Calero is really about — and it's the opposite of the pop-psych party trick everyone quotes. This is a full summary and analysis of the classic body-language book: gesture clusters, congruence, and why "crossed arms = defensive" is exactly the mistake the authors spent the whole book warning against.
