## Structural Root Causes

1. **Keyword Collision without Narrative Context:** A stray word like `"think"` in scene 10 triggered Daniel Kahneman's `"SYSTEM 1 VS SYSTEM 2"` callout in an ancient Greek dialogue about a Spartan purge.
2. **Generic Template Spam:** `"CRITICAL DISTINCTION"` appeared 3 times in 14 scenes whenever the copywriter couldn't find a punchline.
3. **Emotional Blindness:** Glaucon celebrated with open arms and a wide smile while the narrator was describing psychopathic tyranny collapsing into madness (Scene 4).
4. **Action & Consequence Erasure:** State bloodshed, disarming citizens, kidnapping Polemarchus, and executing Socrates were all rendered as two philosophers politely having an afternoon stroll.
