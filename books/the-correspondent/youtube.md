This is a deep analysis of The Correspondent by Virginia Evans: Sybil Van Antwerp, 73, a retired lawyer in Annapolis, Maryland, who writes more than she speaks. We cover her garden club fight, the romance that unfolds almost entirely on the page, the online correspondent, her brother Felix and her neighbour Harry, the gap between how she presents herself and how others describe her, and the ending, which asks who was really writing to whom.
