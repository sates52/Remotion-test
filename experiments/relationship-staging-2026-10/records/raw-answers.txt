Q1. Who controls whom, and by what means?
The Golds (three yellow figures on a raised dark-gold platform, top) control the Reds (five red figures on a lower, paler platform, bottom right) by telling them a story/lie: a Gold speaks "A STORY ABOUT THE FUTURE", it is stamped "LIE" and an arrow carries it down to the Reds, each of whom then holds a speech box labelled "LIE". So control is by deception/propaganda, not force. A Gold sits higher, so they are the ruling class.
Confidence: high on Golds controlling Reds via a lie; medium on the exact nature (promise of a future that is false).
Unsure: whether the Reds believe the lie or merely receive it.

Q2. What is the gun's role?
The gun is shown inside a dashed box crossed out with a red X, captioned "NOT WITH GUNS". So the gun is the thing NOT used: control is not by weapons/force. It appears only in frames 2-3 as a negated element.
Confidence: high.
Unsure: whether the crossed-out sign means "they do not use guns" or "guns are forbidden/absent"; I read it as the former.

Q3. What does any enclosure, dome or container mean?
There is no dome or enclosure. The only containers are the dashed square around the crossed-out gun (a "prohibited / not this" sign) and speech bubbles. The platforms are bases, not enclosures.
Confidence: high.
Unsure: nothing significant.

Q4. What happens from frame 1 to frame 5?
Frame 1: Golds (3, upper left, larger, on a dark platform) and Reds (5, lower right, smaller, on a pale platform) are introduced with labels. Frame 2: a crossed-out gun appears labelled "NOT WITH GUNS". Frame 3: a Gold speaks a bubble "A STORY ABOUT THE FUTURE". Frame 4: the Gold speech is stamped "LIE" and an arrow sends it to the Reds, who each now hold a "LIE" bubble; the Reds' platform has darkened and a faded sad-looking red-clad figure appears at left. Frame 5: the scene is replaced by a close-up of a single worried/sad dark-haired person in red overalls (a Red worker), tilted, looking anxious.
Confidence: high on sequence; medium on the interpretation of the final close-up (the human cost / a Red's view of being lied to).
Unsure: whether the Reds' platform darkening means they sink or get burdened.
