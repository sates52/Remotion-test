## Description
A grave over a bill of sale: that is the choice at the heart of Beloved by Toni Morrison.

This is a deep analysis of Beloved: the characters, the turning points, the themes, and the argument the novel makes about ledgers, love and property. We cover what happens, what it means, and the reading most people miss, and we take on the book’s hardest criticism head-on.

Best for readers studying Beloved for class or a book club, and anyone who wants the real takeaway. Spoilers included.
