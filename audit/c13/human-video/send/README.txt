Muted video study — instructions for the rater

1. Unzip this folder anywhere.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge or Firefox (double-click). The videos are in the clips folder next to it.
3. Watch each pair of silent videos and answer the questions (about 40 minutes).
4. Press "Download my answers" at the end and send the downloaded answers-<name>.json back.
Please do not discuss the videos with other raters until everyone has finished.
