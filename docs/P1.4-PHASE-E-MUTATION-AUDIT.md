## 3. Ledger design (E1 — measurement first, zero behavior change)

A shared, tiny helper (`scripts/lib/post-plan-ledger.js`, ~40 lines) that every post-plan mutator call-site wraps its writes with:

```jsonc
// appended to the scene's own record — travels with the config, firewall-ignorable
"_visualStrategy.postPlanDeltas": [
  { "engine": "stagnation", "pass": 2, "field": "shot", "from": "diorama", "to": "split",
    "reason": "STAGNATION_MITIGATION remedy #4 (3 identical visual states)",
    "sigBefore": {…semanticSignature}, "sigAfter": {…},
    "semanticChange": false,          // sig comparison, computed by the helper
    "reviewRequired": false }         // true iff the delta moved a strategy-staged or requirement-bearing field
]
```

- Engines keep their behavior EXACTLY — they only announce what they already do (record, not prevent).
- `semanticSignature` (PHASE B) already exists; the helper computes before/after per touched scene, so the invariant "a strategy may change representation, never meaning" becomes CHECKABLE per mutation, not just plan-vs-final.
- A per-run summary lands in the plan output (`⚠ N post-plan mutations; M overrode strategy staging; K flagged review`) and a sidecar `data/post-plan-mutation-audit.json` for cross-book stats.
- The existing capture stays as the backstop: even un-instrumented mutations remain visible as staging-tag ⇄ final-scene mismatches (P1.5 proved this detection works).

**Success test for E1:** re-run the WWL chain → every one of the 20 overwrites carries `{engine, pass, field, from, to, reason}` with `semanticChange:false` (shot swaps only) and `reviewRequired:true` (they override strategy staging). Any mutation the ledger missed = instrument gap, found deterministically.

## 4. Policy options for E2 (decided AFTER E1 data — not implemented now)

| Option | Meaning | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| **preserve** | strategy-staged fields are off-limits to mutators | mutators' retention goal may degrade (they changed those shots for a reason) |
| **allow+record** | mutator wins, ledger + `reviewRequired:true`; the firewall's requirement re-check keeps the UNMET visible | honest, no behavior risk; books may FAIL more until capability staging is preserved by the mutators themselves |
| **revert-with-record** | restore the staged value, keep the ledger entry | the mutator's own goal silently undone — worst of both, likely wrong |

Recommendation: E1 (ledger) → look at the real pass/field/reason distribution → then likely "allow+record" for non-staged fields and a narrow "preserve" only for `shot` staged by a measured-safe fallback (the mutators have 13 other scenes to diversify; they do not need THIS scene's shot).
